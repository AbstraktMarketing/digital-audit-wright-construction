J&D Financial is running 3 of 4 cookies before any visitor has consented — including 2 Squarespace analytical tracking cookies (ss_cvr, ss_cvt) that fire immediately on page load, plus 1 unclassified persistent cookie flagged as the highest GDPR risk by the scanner. No consent management platform (CMP) is in place. Twelve states — including California, Colorado, Texas, and Connecticut — legally require websites to detect and honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signals automatically, before a user takes any action. These laws apply based on where the visitor is located, not where the company operates. With offices in California and clients nationwide, J&D Financial’s exposure spans the most actively enforced jurisdictions. California has issued seven-figure fines for exactly this configuration; Ford Motor Company was required in March 2026 to audit every tracking technology on its properties for GPC non-compliance.

Why Specific Pages Beat General Pages

A single homepage section on “factoring” can’t rank for a staffing company, an apparel manufacturer, and a wholesale distributor at the same time, because Google and AI engines are trying to match a specific question to a specific answer. This is also how AI search works: a prompt like “invoice factoring for staffing companies” triggers several related sub-searches, and the engine assembles its answer from whichever pages address each piece most directly. Dedicated pages for each service and industry give Google and AI systems a clear, specific answer — instead of a homepage that mentions everything and owns nothing.
