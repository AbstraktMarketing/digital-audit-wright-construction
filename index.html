PCI Services is running 2 Google Ads tracking cookies and 2 Google Analytics cookies with no consent management platform in place — and all 8 cookies on the site fire before any visitor interaction. Twelve states, including California, Colorado, Texas, and Connecticut, legally require websites to detect and honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signals automatically. These laws apply based on where the visitor is located, not where the company operates. Any U.S.-facing website is in scope. California has issued seven-figure fines for exactly this configuration; Ford Motor Company was required in March 2026 to audit every tracking technology on its properties for GPC non-compliance. An additional 4 unclassified cookies (CallTrackingMetrics) add further exposure.

Why Specific Pages Beat General Pages

PCI's current site has a homepage and three broad service sections. A Facilities Manager at a pharmaceutical company searching “pharmaceutical piping contractor Cincinnati” is not going to find their answer on a page called “Custom Piping.” Their current SEO program is structured around B2C patterns — broad service pages, no industry specificity. PCI's ICPs are B2B industrial buyers with specific verticals, long procurement cycles, and high project values. The keyword strategy needs to match that reality.

The reference model is Hartwig Mechanical (hartwigmechanical.com — an AMG-built site): separate pages for Commercial, Industrial, and Design-Build service types, then a full “Industries” section with dedicated pages for Manufacturing, Healthcare, Food Processing, and more. That structure is why Google can match a food plant facilities director's search to a specific answer. PCI has the client roster to back those pages — P&G, Pfizer, Duke Energy, Koch Foods, Sam Adams — it just hasn't built the structure that puts those capabilities in front of the right buyer at the right moment.

The Local Advantage
Cincinnati-specific searches have lower competition and higher buyer intent than national terms.

A buyer typing “commercial HVAC contractor Cincinnati” is ready to get bids — not researching a topic. National terms attract informational traffic and national competition. Local geo-modified terms filter to buyers in PCI's service area, with keyword difficulty 2–5 points lower on average and a much shorter path to the top 3. PCI's verified Cincinnati GBP, physical address, and 100+ year local operating history give it a structural head start over any out-of-market competitor ranking nationally. That advantage only pays off if the pages exist.
